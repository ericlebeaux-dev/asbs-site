About ASBS

People. Process. Solutions. Results.

American Standard Business Solutions, LLC is based in Katy, Texas and serves Greater Houston with janitorial, pressure washing, and facility support. Office, janitorial, paper, and MRO supplies ship nationwide. Consulting conversations are also available nationwide.

The company is a Texas Veteran-Owned Business, verified by the Texas Veterans Commission, and is Texas CMBL registered. Public procurement identifiers already published by ASBS are UEI MXEQNQLXY1G9 and CAGE 20KS7.

Mission

Keep a customer's facility clean, stocked, and running, and give a straight answer when they ask for help with procurement, logistics, or contracting readiness. One accountable company, with direct access to the owner.

Leadership

Eric LeBeaux

Founder & Managing Director

Army veteran · Katy / Greater Houston

Eric is an Army logistics leader with more than 20 years of experience running large-scale operations, managing supply chains and budgets, and leading teams. He holds an MBA and is trained in Lean Six Sigma. Today he leads a team of dedicated professionals who serve commercial clients across Greater Houston.

ASBS runs on the principles Eric learned in uniform: integrity in every quote and invoice, accountability for every task, attention to detail on every walkthrough and delivery, and a mission-first focus on keeping your facility clean, stocked, and running. He leads by example, so the standard he sets is the standard your business gets.
