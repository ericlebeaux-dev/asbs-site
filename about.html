About

People, Process, Solutions, Results.

American Standard Business Solutions, LLC is a veteran-owned and minority-owned consulting firm. Serving Greater Houston, Greater San Antonio, Greater Austin, and Greater DFW, including communities within about 50 miles of each. The work is advice and hands-on preparation for growth, expansion, readiness, and contracting opportunities in government and commercial markets.

The company is a Texas Veteran-Owned Business, verified by the Texas Veterans Commission, and is Texas CMBL registered. Public procurement identifiers already published by ASBS are UEI MXEQNQLXY1G9 and CAGE 20KS7.

How the work is led

ASBS is veteran-led and minority-led. Leadership brings more than twenty years of military service to our nation and built this company on service, integrity, discipline, and focus. Those values guide the advice: say what is true, write down the next step, and leave the decision with the client.

Clients can reach the business directly at (281) 954-5167.

What you can expect

  • A free introductory consultation before any paid scope.
  • A written agreement before paid work starts.
  • Opportunity notes a person prepared, with the source attached.
  • No promise of an award, a certification, financing, or an agency’s approval.
