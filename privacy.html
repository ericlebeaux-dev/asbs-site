Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us, such as your name, organization, email, phone number, and details of your request when you submit a quote form, contact us, or make a payment.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to inquiries, prepare quotes, fulfill orders and services, process payments, and keep business records. We do not sell your personal information.

Payments

Online payments are processed by our third-party payment processor. We do not store full card or bank account numbers on our systems.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us operate (such as payment processing, form handling, and email), or when required by law.

Security and retention

We take reasonable measures to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for business, legal, and tax purposes.

Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your information by contacting info@americanstandardbusinesssolutions.com.

Changes

We may update this policy and will post the revised version on this page.

Draft addendum for owner and legal review (October 26, 2026)

This addendum is a draft for the owner and the owner's legal reviewer. It is not legal advice and it is not in effect until the owner approves it.

  • Consultation bookings, client intake, contact messages, and vendor registrations are stored on the ASBS server that receives the form. They are not displayed publicly.
  • Vendor files such as a redacted W-9, certificate of insurance, or license are stored outside the public website and can be downloaded only by someone signed in to the private dashboard.
  • ASBS asks customers not to send Social Security numbers, passwords, one-time codes, or bank account numbers. A form that includes those fields is rejected.
  • If Google Calendar is connected, a booking can create an event on the owner's calendar. The scheduling system is set not to email the customer an invitation.
  • The website does not turn on a consulting-package checkout. The existing Pay Invoice link is only for an invoice ASBS has already sent.
  • First-party page events, when the staging server is running, record the page path and a mobile, tablet, or desktop label. They do not record the form contents. Google Analytics is not connected until a measurement ID is added.
