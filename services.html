Services

Practical help, named as deliverables.

ASBS works as a consulting partner. Opportunity review is done by a person, with sources you can open yourself. This is not an automated matching platform, and it is not a promise that an agency or a customer will say yes. Serving Greater Houston, Greater San Antonio, Greater Austin, and Greater DFW, including communities within about 50 miles of each.

Free consultation How an engagement works

01

Growth and expansion

For an owner who needs the next stage described in plain language.

  • A working session on the market, offer, and capacity you already have.
  • A short list of constraints, in the order they block growth.
  • A recommended sequence for the next quarter, written so your team can use it.

02

Business readiness

For a company that wants to know if it is ready to pursue a class of work.

  • A readiness review of registrations, a capability narrative, insurance and bonding questions, and whether you can perform the work.
  • A fix-it list you own. ASBS does not submit registrations unless a written agreement says so.
  • A clear not-yet when the gaps are too large to ignore.

03

Government contracting opportunities

For federal, state, or local notices you are willing to look at seriously.

  • A human review of specific notices, with the source link kept on the note.
  • A pursue or pass recommendation and the reasons.
  • You decide, and you submit in your own name. No award is guaranteed.

04

Commercial contracting opportunities

For private-sector bids, proposals, and customer opportunities you bring or ask ASBS to examine with you.

  • The same pursue or pass note used for public-sector work.
  • A list of gaps that would make a response weak.
  • No claim of a complete national database of private opportunities.

What ASBS will not imply

  • Eligibility, financing, a certification, or a contract award.
  • That a free consultation is a purchase.
  • That a software platform is already matching buyers and sellers for you.
