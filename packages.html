Quick Bid Review

$249

One solicitation. This is a paid written review, not a charge for the free introductory call.

Intended customer

A business that already has one solicitation and wants a documented bid or no-bid recommendation.

Included deliverables

  • Review of one solicitation the customer provides, up to 50 pages.
  • A written bid or no-bid recommendation with the reasons.
  • A short list of obvious gaps.
  • One round of clarification questions.

Limitations

  • Does not include writing or submitting a proposal, or calling the agency.
  • A longer solicitation, later amendments, or a second notice is a separate quote.
  • Does not guarantee that a bid would be responsive or awarded.

Delivery

3 business days after ASBS has the solicitation and intake.

Customer responsibilities

Provide the solicitation, accurate registration information, and answers within two business days.

Upgrade

Contractor Starter, or a separately quoted proposal-support engagement.

Contractor Starter

$599

A defined readiness package. Not an open-ended retainer.

Intended customer

A small business preparing to pursue government work and wanting a starting checklist.

Included deliverables

  • A readiness checklist based on documents the customer provides.
  • A NAICS and NIGP discussion for the customer to confirm. Not an official classification.
  • A capability-statement outline the customer edits.
  • One 45-minute working session.

Limitations

  • ASBS does not submit SAM.gov or other registrations under this package.
  • No proposal writing and no guarantee of certification or approval.
  • Passwords and login codes are not collected.

Delivery

10 business days after intake.

Customer responsibilities

The customer remains the owner of every government account and makes the business decisions.

Registration Accelerator

$1,299

Guided preparation. The customer submits. Agency fees are not included.

Intended customer

A business ready to prepare SAM.gov and one state or local vendor registration.

Included deliverables

  • A SAM.gov preparation checklist.
  • A checklist for one state or local registry the customer selects.
  • Up to two review cycles of drafts the customer prepares.
  • A NAICS and NIGP worksheet.
  • Two working sessions of up to 45 minutes each.

Limitations

  • The customer submits and remains the account owner.
  • ASBS does not store government usernames, passwords, or one-time codes.
  • Agency processing time and decisions are outside this scope.
  • Does not guarantee approval or a contract award.

Delivery

15 business days of ASBS preparation. Agency clocks are extra.

Customer responsibilities

Provide accurate information, redact Social Security numbers, and complete the customer's own submissions.

Full-Service Contractor Launch

$2,499

A capped launch. Not unlimited capture or proposal writing.

Intended customer

A business that wants a structured first month of public-sector selling after the free consultation.

Included deliverables

  • Everything in Registration Accelerator.
  • Screening of up to 10 publicly posted opportunities.
  • A bid or no-bid recommendation on up to 3 of those.
  • One capability-statement draft for the customer to approve.
  • A 30-day action plan.

Limitations

  • Screening stops at 10 notices. Bid or no-bid stops at 3.
  • Full proposal writing and agency submission are not included.
  • No bonding, financing, insurance placement, or legal review.
  • No award, certification, or approval is guaranteed.

Delivery

30 business days after intake.

Customer responsibilities

Provide timely decisions, confirm that past-performance statements are accurate, and submit anything in the customer's own name.

Delivery

Customer responsibilities
