Our Services

Cleaning services in Greater Houston and Katy, plus supplies shipped anywhere in the U.S. Here is what we can do for you.

Janitorial & Commercial Cleaning

A clean building makes a great first impression and keeps everyone healthier. We clean offices, common areas, and restrooms in Greater Houston and Katy on a schedule that works for you, whether that means during the day or after everyone goes home.

We also pressure wash sidewalks, entrances, and building exteriors so the outside looks as good as the inside.

What's included

  • Office and common-area cleaning: dusting, wiping surfaces, and vacuuming
  • Restroom cleaning, sanitizing, and restocking
  • Floor care: sweeping, mopping, and general floor upkeep
  • Trash and recycling removal
  • Scheduled day or night service
  • Pressure washing of sidewalks, entrances, and building exteriors

Janitorial & Sanitation Supplies

Keep your supply closet stocked without the hassle of shopping around. Tell us what you use, and we will put together an order and ship it to you, anywhere in the country.

What's included

  • Cleaning chemicals and disinfectants
  • Trash liners and can liners
  • Paper towels and toilet tissue
  • Soap, towel, and tissue dispensers
  • Personal protective equipment (PPE) such as gloves
  • Mops, brooms, and other cleaning tools

Paper & Office Supplies

From copy paper to breakroom basics, we help offices, schools, and agencies keep the everyday essentials on hand. One vendor, one order, delivered nationwide.

What's included

  • Copy paper and printing supplies
  • Toner and ink
  • Pens, pencils, and writing supplies
  • File folders and binders
  • Breakroom paper goods: cups, napkins, and plates
  • General desk and office basics

Industrial MRO Supplies

Maintenance, repair, and operations (MRO) supplies keep a facility running day to day. Send us your list, and we will source the items and get them to your site.

What's included

  • Shop towels and industrial wipers
  • Gloves and other safety supplies
  • Facility maintenance consumables
  • Restocking orders for maintenance teams
  • Help sourcing hard-to-find items on request

Other Chemical Products

Beyond everyday janitorial chemicals, we supply other cleaning and chemical products that facilities use. Let us know what you need, and we will quote it.

What's included

  • General-purpose cleaners
  • Degreasers and glass cleaners
  • Sanitizers and disinfectants
  • Floor-care products
  • Hand soap

Facility Support

Need help beyond cleaning? We can bundle cleaning with other support tasks in Greater Houston and Katy, so you have one point of contact.

What's included

  • Grounds upkeep
  • Minor maintenance
  • Mail room and reception support
  • Moving and supply-room support
